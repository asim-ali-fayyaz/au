Introduction

Mission Statement

The department mission is to provide quality education and produce highly skilled professionals in the areas of CS, IT, and Bioinformatics. The graduates are proficient at solving the local and global research and development problems effectively with social responsibility. The department stands committed to creating a strong relationship with industry and society to address contemporary and future challenges.

What graduates achieve

Program Objectives (POs)

PO 1

Computing knowledge, skills, and creativity

Demonstrate computing knowledge and skills with a strong focus on innovation, entrepreneurship and life-long learning.

PO 2

Ethics and social responsibility

Have ethical and moral values along with a keen sense of social responsibility.

PO 3

Communication and Leadership Skills

Have effective communication skills required in the computing profession, as a team player or a leader for industry and society.

Chair Department

Message from the Chair

AF
Dr. Ashfaq Hussain Farooqi Associate Professor · Chair, Department of Computer Science

Welcome to the Department of Computer Science at Air University, Islamabad!

I welcome you all to the Department of Computer Science at Air University, Islamabad. Computing has become more accessible rather ubiquitous than ever before it has been in the past. It has almost taken everything under its novelty starting from simple voice messages to natural language and speech processing, helping machines to mimic vision ability that sometimes surpasses the humans and the list goes on.

Computer Science is one of the leading Departments of Air University and offers Undergraduate, Master, and Ph.D. programs. All of the offered programs are duly recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) and NCEAC, Pakistan. The Department strongly believes in industry and academia collaboration and we have been continuously revising our curriculum by adding different advanced courses and updating courses outlines with consultations of industrial experts and practitioners.

Department of Computer Science has been producing high-quality graduates for the past several years and aims at producing more in years to come. Our graduates have been working at various internationally acclaimed organizations such as Netsol, SBP, Huawei, Teradata, IBM, Microsoft, and contributing at many local companies.

  • Netsol
  • SBP
  • Huawei
  • Teradata
  • IBM
  • Microsoft

Our faculty have earned degrees from reputable national and international universities. The faculty has published a significant number of research articles and won several funded projects. Department has secured around Rs. 73.0m in funding in 2018 through grants from the Planning Commission Govt. of Pakistan.

Rs. 73.0m in funding secured in 2018 through grants from the Planning Commission, Govt. of Pakistan.

It is an exciting time for Computer Science & IT Researchers, Teachers, and Students who are capable of making a difference in the world and emerge as developers, thinkers, and innovators. Computer Science will always be a field of study of choice with a lot of opportunities in Pakistan and abroad.

We here at the Department of Computer Science are providing conducive environment that will help you to learn, grow, and serve the community. We have a highly qualified faculty which are enlightening students to open new horizons of knowledge. I wish you the best of luck in all of your academic endeavors here at the Department of Computer Science at Air University.

Dr. Ashfaq Hussain Farooqi Associate Professor
Chair (Department of Computer Science)
